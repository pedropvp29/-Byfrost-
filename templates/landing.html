✳ Frequently asked questions

Still have questions?

What is Byfrost?

Byfrost is a platform designed to connect donors, people who discover donation opportunities, businesses, nonprofits, and collection points.

Do I need something to donate?

Not at all. You can share places with available resources, spread the word about campaigns, and connect people with organizations.

Can a nonprofit create an account?

Yes. Choose the nonprofit option when signing up. Account approval and organization verification depend on the rules implemented in the Flask backend.

How do collection points work?

Collection points are places that can help receive and organize items according to the campaigns and activities listed on the platform.

Does the platform process donations automatically?

This landing page introduces the service and provides access to your account. Posts, approvals, logistics, and authentication depend on the features implemented in the Byfrost backend.
