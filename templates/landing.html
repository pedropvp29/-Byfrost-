A few good questions

Everything starts with knowing.

What is Byfrost?

A community-focused social platform where nonprofits publish requests and stories, and people and businesses discover ways to contribute.

Who can publish donation requests?

Only registered nonprofit accounts can create requests for needed items and define collection points. Individuals and businesses can respond with offers and contributions.

How does the personalized feed work?

Your interactions and interests help rank relevant posts and nonprofit needs. You can control topics, hide posts and reset your interests inside your account.

How do I know a donation was completed?

The nonprofit confirms completed physical donations. Pending offers are not counted as confirmed contributions. The platform is a prototype and nonprofit verification may be incomplete.

Does Byfrost transfer money?

Not in this prototype. Any financial contribution demonstration is clearly labeled as a simulation and does not process real payments.
